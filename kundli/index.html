kundli questions, answered

frequently asked

What is a kundli?

A kundli, also called a janam kundli or birth chart, is a map of where the planets were at the moment and place you were born. It shows the twelve houses, the twelve zodiac signs, the nine planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu) and the nakshatra, or birth star, of each. It is calculated from astronomical data, so two correct kundlis for the same birth details are always identical.

What is Lahiri ayanamsa?

Lahiri, also called Chitrapaksha, is the standard Indian way of setting where the zodiac starts. Western astrology starts the zodiac from the March equinox. Indian astrology starts it from the fixed stars, with the star Spica at exactly 180°. The gap between the two is about 24° today and grows slowly each year. India adopted Lahiri as its national standard in 1955.

This is why a vedic chart usually puts your Sun one sign earlier than a western horoscope does.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

Tick the unknown-time box and we use sunrise on your birth date. Your Moon sign, birth star and the sign of every planet stay reliable, except in the rare case that the Moon changes sign that day.

Your rising sign (lagna), your houses and your dasha dates become approximate. The rising sign changes about every two hours, and dasha dates can shift by months. shastra tells you this on the chart.

Why is my vedic sun sign different from my western one?

The two systems start the zodiac from different points. Western astrology uses the March equinox. Vedic astrology uses the fixed stars. The Earth's axis wobbles slowly, so these two points have drifted about 24° apart, a little under one full sign.

So someone born in late March is Aries in a western horoscope and usually Pisces in a vedic one. Neither is wrong. They define the start of the zodiac differently.

What is a nakshatra?

A nakshatra, or birth star, is one of 27 equal parts of the zodiac. Each is about as wide as the distance the Moon travels in a day, and each is split into four quarters, called padas.

Your Moon's nakshatra is the most used value in Indian astrology. It sets the order of your dashas, kundli matching compares it, and it traditionally gives the first syllable of a child's name.

What is vimshottari dasha?

Vimshottari dasha divides life into periods ruled by the planets, adding up to 120 years. Each planet's main period, or mahadasha, has a fixed length: Ketu 7 years, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17.

The exact position of your Moon at birth decides which period you start in and how much of it was already over. Each main period is split into shorter ones, called antardashas, in the same order.
