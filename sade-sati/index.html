the three phases

two and a half years each, in a fixed order.

phase one

rising · 12th from the moon

Saturn enters the sign before your Moon sign. Classically associated with expenditure, endings and things being wound down. Traditionally read as the beginning of a slowing, not of a crisis.

phase two

peak · on the moon

Saturn occupies your Moon sign itself. The phase most often written about, and classically linked to the self, the body and mental weather — which is precisely why it is also the one most often sold against.

phase three

setting · 2nd from the moon

Saturn moves into the sign after your Moon sign. Associated with family, speech and resources. The tail of the passage rather than its own event.

the phases can appear to overlap in a computed timeline, and that is not an error: Saturn retrogrades. It commonly enters the next sign, reverses back into the previous one for a few months, then enters again for good. shastra shows each phase from its first entry to its final exit and tells you when a re-entry happened, rather than picking one date and hiding the other two.

a note we think matters

on what sade sati is not.

Search for sade sati and most of what comes back is calibrated to worry you, because worry is what converts. The structure is consistent: establish that a difficult period is coming, quantify it vaguely, then offer a gemstone, a puja, a yantra or a paid consultation as the way out.

The astronomy underneath is not in dispute. Saturn takes about 29.5 years to go round the zodiac, so it spends about seven and a half years in any three consecutive signs. Roughly three of the twelve moon signs are in sade sati at any moment — which means about a quarter of every population on earth is in it right now, including a great many people having an unremarkable decade.

Classical sources describe the period in terms of slowing, consolidation, and being made to do things properly. That is a long way from what the market has made of it. shastra computes the dates, tells you which phase you are in, and sells nothing that claims to change any of it — no remedies, ever. sade sati questions, answered

frequently asked

What is sade sati?

Sade sati is the period when Saturn transits the three signs centred on your natal Moon sign — the 12th, the 1st and the 2nd. Saturn takes roughly two and a half years per sign, so the whole passage lasts about seven and a half years, which is what the name means in Hindi.

Because Saturn's orbit is eccentric its apparent speed varies, so real episodes run between about six and a half and nine years rather than exactly 7.5. It recurs roughly every 29.5 years — Saturn's orbital period — so most people see it two or three times.

How do I know if sade sati is running for me?

It depends on two things only: your natal Moon sign (your rashi) and where Saturn is now. If Saturn currently occupies the sign before your Moon sign, your Moon sign itself, or the sign after it, sade sati is running.

Your lagna, your sun sign and your birth time do not enter this calculation at all, which is why an unknown birth time barely affects the answer.

What are the three phases?

Rising, when Saturn is in the 12th from your Moon; peak, when Saturn is in the same sign as your Moon; and setting, when Saturn is in the 2nd from your Moon. Each lasts roughly two and a half years.

Classical texts associate them loosely with different areas — the 12th with expenditure and endings, the 1st with the self and the body, the 2nd with family and resources — but they are phases of a transit, not a schedule of events.

Is sade sati always bad?

No, and the assumption that it is has been commercially useful to a lot of people. Sade sati is a transit — Saturn passing through three particular signs relative to where the Moon was when you were born.

Classical texts describe it as a period of slowing, consolidation and demanded discipline rather than a sentence. And it arrives for about a quarter of the population at any given moment, since roughly three of the twelve moon signs are in it at once. shastra reports the dates and declines to grade the years.

What is dhaiya or small panoti?

Dhaiya — also called small panoti or kantak shani — is Saturn transiting the 4th or the 8th sign from your natal Moon. Each lasts about two and a half years; dhai varsh is where the name comes from.

It is distinct from sade sati and cannot overlap with it, because the 4th and 8th signs are not among the 12th, 1st and 2nd.

Do I need remedies for sade sati?

shastra does not sell remedies and does not recommend them. The market for gemstones, yantras, pujas and paid rituals aimed at sade sati is large, and it works by first establishing that you should be afraid.

A transit is a position of a planet. It is computable, it has dates, and it ends on its own schedule regardless of what is purchased.

How accurate are these dates?

Ingress dates are computed from the Swiss Ephemeris with Lahiri ayanamsa, resolved to the minute, and verified against published panchang values. Saturn's entry into sidereal Capricorn on 24 January 2020, Aquarius on 17 January 2023 and Pisces on 29 March 2025 are all asserted as tests that must pass before anything ships.

The calculation also captures retrograde re-entries — Saturn entered Aquarius in April 2022, reversed back into Capricorn that July, and re-entered for good in January 2023. Lookup tables often flatten that into one date.

Which sign does sade sati use — my sun sign or my moon sign?

Your Moon sign, always. Vedic astrology treats the Moon sign as the primary reference for transits, and sade sati is defined from it specifically. Using a sun sign — or a western tropical sign — will give you the wrong dates, usually off by about a sign, which is two and a half years of Saturn.
