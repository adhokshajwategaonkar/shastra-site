sade sati calculator

sade sati is the period when Saturn passes through your moon sign and the signs on either side of it. we work out the exact dates, including when Saturn moves back and forth between signs.

the three phases

phase one

rising

saturn enters the sign before your moon sign.

phase two

peak

saturn moves into your moon sign.

phase three

setting

saturn moves into the sign after your moon sign.

before you read the result

at any moment, about a quarter of people are in sade sati: three of the twelve moon signs. it's a planet's position with a start and end date. it doesn't decide how the next seven years will go.

this page gives you the dates. we don't sell anything that claims to cancel it, because nothing can.
