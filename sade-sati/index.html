sade sati calculator

sade sati is the stretch when Saturn passes through the three signs centred on your natal moon: the 12th, the 1st and the 2nd. this one reads Saturn's real ingress dates from the ephemeris, retrograde re-entries included.

the three phases

phase one

rising · 12th from the moon

saturn enters the sign before your moon sign.

phase two

peak · on the moon

saturn occupies your moon sign itself.

phase three

setting · 2nd from the moon

saturn moves into the sign after it.

before you read the result

sade sati is running for about a quarter of everyone alive at any moment: three of the twelve moon signs at a time. it is a planetary position with start and end dates, not a verdict on seven years of your life.

this page will give you the dates and stop there. there is nothing on this site to buy that claims to cancel it, because no such thing exists.

what the dates don't tell you

saturn is one transit. your chart is running several.

sade sati is measured from your moon alone. what it coincides with depends on which vimshottari dasha you are in, where saturn sits in your birth chart to begin with, and what else is moving. shastra's free daily reading works that out against your whole chart every morning. and it will never open with a warning about saturn.
