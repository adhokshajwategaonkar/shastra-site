what this is

why the numbers are trustworthy

the engine is tested against things it cannot fake.

"our astrology is accurate" is a claim anyone can type. so shastra's chart engine ships with a test suite that has to pass before any code does — 196 assertions, and these are the ones that matter:

Spica sits at exactly 180°00′

that is the definition of the Lahiri / Chitrapaksha ayanamsa. if this assertion fails, the ayanamsa isn't Lahiri and every chart computed is quietly wrong. it's the first thing the build checks.

two NASA eclipse times

sun and moon must conjoin at the 2017-08-21 solar eclipse and oppose at the 2018-07-27 lunar eclipse. these checks are independent of any ayanamsa — they test the raw ephemeris itself, against published astronomy.

three published reference charts

Gandhi, Einstein and Modi, asserted against published values rather than values this engine produced. Gandhi's chart matches on all seven classical grahas — sign, nakshatra and dignity.

guna milan is 36 points of code

the full ashtakoota — varna, vashya, tara, yoni, graha maitri, gana, bhakoot, nadi — computed arithmetically, with the classical mangal dosha exemptions applied. a language model is never asked to add up to 36.

positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris (Moshier mode), accurate to about 0.1 arcseconds. a nakshatra pada is 3°20′ wide — so the error is roughly four orders of magnitude smaller than anything that could change a single word of a reading.

questions people actually ask

frequently asked

What is shastra?

shastra is a vedic astrology service that computes your chart deterministically with the Swiss Ephemeris and explains it in plain language. The daily reading is free and arrives over WhatsApp when you message first each morning. Deep reports are flat one-time payments — ₹99 for career, ₹149 for compatibility — with no subscription, no per-minute billing, and no remedies for sale.

How is this different from a per-minute astrology consultation app?

Per-minute apps charge roughly ₹20–₹50 per minute while a human types, so a twenty-minute conversation costs ₹400–₹1,000 and the total is unknown until it ends. shastra charges a flat one-time price per report, displayed before you pay. It also refuses the categories that model depends on: no predictions about death, illness, pregnancy or accidents, no manufactured urgency, and no gemstones, yantras or paid remedies.

Does an AI write the astrology, or is the maths real?

Both parts are separated on purpose. Every planetary position, nakshatra, dasha date and guna milan point is computed by the Swiss Ephemeris in code — a language model never calculates a position. A language model only writes the interpretation, and it receives the computed chart as structured data, never your birth date, birth place, email or phone number.

Is the daily reading really free, or free-for-now?

Free, and structurally so. In India a business-initiated WhatsApp template message costs the sender about ₹1.09, which is roughly ₹33 per user per month for a daily send — that product could not stay free. A reply sent inside a conversation you started costs nothing. So the reading is a reply, you send the first message, and the economics work at any number of users.

Which ayanamsa and house system does shastra use?

Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, whole-sign houses, mean lunar node — the standard conventions in Indian vedic astrology. Lahiri is defined by placing the star Spica at exactly 180° of sidereal longitude, and that is asserted as a hard test: if it ever drifted, the build fails before anything ships.

What happens to my birth details?

They're stored to compute your chart, and used for nothing else. They are never sold or shared with advertisers. The language model that writes your reading never receives them — it sees only the computed chart, with the identifying fields stripped before the call is made. You can delete your account and every row attached to it at any time; see the privacy policy.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The daily reading works entirely inside WhatsApp. The web app opens in a browser and can be added to your home screen if you want the full chart, the report reader and compatibility — but nothing requires an install.

Is shastra available outside India?

The chart engine works for any birth location and time zone on earth, and the free tools on this site are open to everyone. Paid reports are currently priced and processed in Indian rupees over UPI; international payment is planned but not live.
