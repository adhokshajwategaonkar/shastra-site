why trust it

check the maths yourself.

anyone can say their astrology is accurate. before any update goes live, it has to pass 214 automated checks. here are three you can check yourself:

the star Spica sits at exactly 180°

that’s how Lahiri, the standard zodiac in Indian astrology, is defined. if this check fails, every chart would be slightly wrong, so it’s the first thing we test.

two NASA eclipse times

the sun and moon must line up at the solar eclipse of 21 August 2017, and sit opposite each other at the lunar eclipse of 27 July 2018. this tests our planet positions against published astronomy.

three published birth charts

Gandhi, Einstein and Modi. we test against their published charts, not our own results. Gandhi’s matches on all seven classical planets: sign, birth star and strength.

questions people ask

frequently asked

What is shastra?

shastra is a free vedic astrology service. We work out your birth chart with the Swiss Ephemeris, the same planet data astronomers use, and explain it in plain words. Message on WhatsApp in the morning and you get a short reading for the day. Longer reports cost ₹99 for career and ₹149 for compatibility, paid once. There is no subscription, no per-minute charge and no remedies for sale.

How is this different from a per-minute astrology consultation app?

Per-minute apps charge about ₹20–₹50 a minute, so a twenty-minute chat costs ₹400–₹1,000, and you don’t know the total until it ends. shastra charges one fixed price per report, and you see it before you pay. We also make no predictions about death, illness, pregnancy or accidents, create no fake urgency, and sell no gemstones, yantras or other remedies.

Does an AI write the astrology, or is the maths real?

Both, and they are kept apart. Code works out every planet position, birth star, dasha date and guna milan point using the Swiss Ephemeris. AI never does that calculation. AI only writes the reading, using the finished chart. It never sees your birth date, birth place, email or phone number.

Is the daily reading really free?

Yes. When a business sends the first WhatsApp message, it pays about ₹1.09 each time, or about ₹33 a month per person. That could not stay free. A reply in a chat you started costs nothing. So you message first, the reading is a reply, and it stays free however many people use it.

Which ayanamsa and house system does shastra use?

Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, whole-sign houses and the mean lunar node. These are the standard choices in Indian vedic astrology. Lahiri places the star Spica at exactly 180°, and we test for that: if it ever drifts, the update doesn’t go live.

What happens to my birth details?

We use them to work out your chart and nothing else. We never sell them or share them with advertisers. The AI that writes your reading never sees them: it only gets the finished chart, with anything that identifies you removed. You can delete your account and all its data at any time. See the privacy policy.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The daily reading works inside WhatsApp. The web app opens in a browser, and you can add it to your home screen if you want your full chart, reports and compatibility, but you never have to install anything.

Is shastra available outside India?

Yes. Charts work for any birth place and time zone, and the free tools here are open to everyone. Paid reports are in Indian rupees over UPI for now. International payment is planned but not live.
