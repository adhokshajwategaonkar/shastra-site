Refund Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

The short version

Not happy with a report? Email us within 7 days and we'll refund it. No questions, no forms, no "please explain why."

That's the actual policy. The rest of this page is detail, not conditions.

How to get a refund

Email adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com from the address on your account, within 7 days of purchase. Tell us which report. That's all we need.

You don't have to give a reason. If you want to tell us what was wrong, we'd like to know — it's how the product gets better — but it isn't a condition.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge within 48 hours.
  • We process the refund within 5 working days of acknowledging.
  • The money goes back to the same payment method you used — UPI, card, or netbanking. We cannot send it anywhere else; that's a payment-processor rule, not ours.
  • Once processed, your bank typically takes 5–7 working days to show it. That part is out of our hands.
  • We'll email you when it's sent.

You keep any free readings and your streak. Refunding a report doesn't affect your account.

If something went wrong on our side

If you were charged and never received your report — generation failed, the link never arrived, anything — that isn't a refund request, it's a bug. Email us and we will either deliver the report or refund you in full, whichever you prefer, and the 7-day window does not apply. You will never be out of pocket for something we failed to deliver.

If you were charged twice for the same thing, tell us and we'll refund the duplicate immediately. Again, no window.

After 7 days

Reports are generated individually for your chart at the moment you buy them, so past the 7-day window we don't refund as a rule.

But we're one person, not a policy department. If something genuinely went wrong — you were charged for the wrong thing, the report was for the wrong chart, you bought it twice by accident — email us anyway. We'd rather sort it out than be technically correct.

What we don't refund

  • Free readings. There's nothing to refund.
  • Reports bought more than 7 days ago, absent one of the situations above.

That's the entire list.

No subscriptions to cancel

shastra has no subscriptions and no auto-renewals. Every purchase is one-time. There is nothing recurring on your account, so there is nothing to cancel — and no cancellation flow to hunt for.

If you want to stop using shastra entirely, delete your account from Settings. That's immediate and permanent.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we handled a refund, email adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com and say so directly — it comes to a person, not a queue. If we still can't resolve it, you retain all your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, including approaching a consumer forum.
