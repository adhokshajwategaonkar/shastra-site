the usual reading

most Indian families treat 18 or more out of 36 as acceptable, 25 or more as strong, and below 18 as weak. nadi and bhakoot carry the most points (8 and 7), so a score that loses both is usually seen differently, even above 18.

matching questions, answered

frequently asked

What is guna milan and how is it scored?

Guna milan, also called ashtakoota milan, is the vedic way of comparing two birth charts for marriage. It scores eight things, called kootas, for 36 points in total: varna 1, vashya 2, tara 3, yoni 4, graha maitri 5, gana 6, bhakoot 7 and nadi 8.

Every score comes from the Moon's birth star and sign in each chart, and nothing else. It is simple arithmetic on fixed tables, so calculators using the same tables give the same number.

How many gunas out of 36 are considered a good match?

The usual reading is 18 or more as acceptable, 25 or more as strong, and below 18 as weak. Nadi (8 points) and bhakoot (7 points) matter most, so a score above 18 that lost both is seen differently from one that lost neither.

These cut-offs are tradition, not part of the calculation. shastra shows the score and the breakdown. It doesn't judge the relationship.

What is nadi dosha?

Nadi dosha means both people's Moon birth stars fall in the same nadi group: aadi, madhya or antya. Nadi is worth 8 of the 36 points, the most of any part, and sharing a group scores zero.

Classical texts list exceptions: both charts having the same Moon sign, the same birth star in different quarters, or birth stars with the same ruling planet. shastra checks these and tells you which apply.

What is mangal dosha, and am I manglik?

Mangal dosha means Mars is in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house counted from a starting point in the chart. Traditions differ on the starting point, so shastra checks all three (the rising sign, the Moon and Venus) and shows each result.

It also applies the traditional exceptions, such as Mars being in its own sign or at its strongest. Mangal dosha is separate from the 36-point score.

Does kundli matching need exact birth times?

Guna milan only needs the Moon's position, which depends far more on the date than the time. So a missing birth time rarely changes the score, and never by much.

Mangal dosha is different. Part of that check uses the rising sign, which changes about every two hours. If the birth time is unknown, that part is approximate, and this page says so.

Is kundli matching required for marriage?

No. It is a common custom in Indian marriages, not a legal requirement. Classical astrology also treats it as one factor among several, not a decision.

Guna milan only compares Moon positions. It knows nothing about two people who already know each other.

Why does my score differ from another website's?

Usually because of one of two settings. Classical sources disagree on parts of vashya (2 points) and graha maitri (5 points), so sites choose differently. A difference of a point or two is normal.

A bigger gap usually means a different ayanamsa, the setting for where the zodiac starts. It can move a Moon into a neighbouring birth star and change several scores at once. shastra uses Lahiri, the Indian standard.
