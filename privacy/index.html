Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

shastra is run by Adhokshaj Wategaonkar, a sole proprietor based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is short on purpose.

Contact for anything in this policy: adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com

1. What we collect

Your birth details — date, time, and place of birth. This is the only way to compute a chart. It is personal data and we treat it as such.

Your WhatsApp phone number — if you use the WhatsApp reading. This is how we know which chart to send back.

Your name — if you give it. Optional.

Account basics — email address if you sign in with one, and a password hash. We never store your password in readable form.

Usage data — which readings you opened, your morning streak, when you last used the app. This runs the streak feature and tells us if anything is broken.

Payment records — what you bought, when, for how much, and a payment reference ID. We never see or store your card number, UPI ID, or bank details. Those go directly to Razorpay, our payment processor, and stay there.

Technical data — IP address, browser type, and device type, kept in server logs for security and debugging.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or anything from your camera or microphone. We do not buy data about you from anyone.

2. Why we collect it, and nothing else

We use your data for exactly these purposes:

WhatWhy
Birth detailsTo compute your chart and generate your readings and reports
Phone numberTo deliver your reading over WhatsApp when you ask for it
EmailTo sign you in, and to send transactional messages (receipts, report-ready notices)
Usage dataTo run your streak, and to find and fix bugs
Payment recordsTo deliver what you paid for, handle refunds, and keep tax records the law requires
Technical logsSecurity, abuse prevention, debugging

We will not use your data for any other purpose without asking you first. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not run ads.

3. Consent

We ask for your birth details at one clear moment, and we tell you what they are for at that moment. Giving them is your consent to us using them for the purposes listed above.

You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account (§6). Withdrawing consent means we stop processing your data — it does not undo readings already generated and sent, and it does not delete the payment records we are legally required to keep.

4. Who else touches your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each one gets only what it needs.

Anthropic (Claude API) — this is the one that deserves a straight answer.

Your readings are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Here is exactly what happens: we compute your chart ourselves, using astronomical software on our own servers. We then send the computed chart — planetary positions, nakshatras, dasha periods, and similar derived values — to Anthropic's API so the model can write the interpretation in plain language.

  • We do not send your name, phone number, or email to Anthropic.
  • We do not send your raw birth date, time, or place — only the chart computed from them.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models, and are deleted from their systems within 30 days.
  • The AI writes the words. It does not calculate your chart, and it never sees your identity.

Razorpay — processes payments. They receive your payment details directly; we receive only a reference and the amount. They are RBI-regulated and hold their own privacy policy.

Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — if you use the WhatsApp reading, Meta processes the messages between you and shastra, and can see your phone number and the message content. This is inherent to using WhatsApp. Your messages are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one. If you would rather Meta not be involved, use the web app instead — everything works there.

Supabase — hosts our database. Render — hosts our servers. Both process data on our instructions only and hold no independent rights to it.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law compels us to.

5. Where your data lives

Our database and servers are hosted with providers whose infrastructure may be located outside India, and Anthropic processes API requests on infrastructure outside India. By using shastra you consent to this transfer. We only use providers who contractually commit to processing data on our instructions and to industry-standard security.

6. Deleting your data — this one actually works

You can delete your account from Settings → Delete account in the app, or by emailing adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com from your registered address.

When you do, we permanently purge your birth details, chart data, generated readings and reports, phone number, email, and usage history from our production database. This is a real deletion, not a flag on a row. It takes effect immediately and completes within 30 days including backups.

It cannot be undone. Your readings and any reports you paid for are gone.

The one exception: we retain minimal payment records — transaction ID, amount, date, and the GST/tax fields the law requires — because Indian tax law requires us to keep them for up to 8 years. These records are stripped of your birth data and are kept for that purpose only.

You can also ask us, at adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com, to:

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and who we've shared it with
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated
  • Complain — to us first, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we don't resolve it

We will respond within 30 days.

7. How long we keep things

  • Active accounts: as long as your account exists.
  • Inactive accounts: if you don't use shastra for [24] months, we email you, and if there's no response within 30 days we delete the account and its data.
  • Deleted accounts: purged immediately, backups within 30 days.
  • Payment/tax records: 8 years, as above.
  • Server logs: 90 days.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Database access is restricted to the application server and filtered by user on every query. Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. API keys and secrets live in environment variables, never in code.

We are a small operation and we will not pretend otherwise: no system is perfectly secure. What we can promise is that we collect as little as possible, so there is less to lose.

If a breach happens that affects your personal data, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India as required by law, and we will tell you directly — what happened, what data was involved, what we're doing about it, and what you should do — within 72 hours of becoming aware.

9. Children

shastra is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it immediately. If you believe a minor has given us their data, email adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com and we will remove it.

10. Cookies and similar

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in, and local storage to remember your preferences. That's it. No advertising cookies. No third-party trackers. No analytics that follow you across the web.

If you turn on push notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription token. You can revoke it in your browser settings at any time.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we'll tell you in the app and by email before it takes effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.

12. Grievance officer

For any complaint about how we handle your data:

Adhokshaj Wategaonkar adhokshaj.pande@gmail.com Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can escalate to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
